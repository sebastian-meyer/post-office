.. title:: Post Office

Documentation
#############

The **Post Office** is a web frontend for a mail server based on `Postfix <https://www.postfix.org/>`_ and
`Dovecot <https://www.dovecot.org/>`_, which store their configuration of domains, mailboxes, aliases and mailing lists
in a `MariaDB <https://mariadb.org/>`_ database.

It allows users to manage their own email account, and administrators to delegate the management of domains and
mailing lists to other users.
