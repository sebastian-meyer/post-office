Logging In

Every enabled mailbox or send-only address can log in to the Post Office with its full email address and the password of its email account. Aliases and mailing lists have no password and can't log in.

After five failed login attempts, further attempts are blocked for 15 minutes.

Roles

What a user can do in the Post Office depends on their role. Every user has at least the role of a regular user, and can additionally be manager of any number of mailing lists and domains.

Users can

  • change their name and password,
  • see the mailing lists they are a member of, and leave them.

List managers can additionally

  • change the settings of their mailing lists,
  • add and remove members of their mailing lists,
  • delete their mailing lists.

Domain managers can additionally

  • create, edit, disable and delete all accounts and mailing lists of their domains,
  • appoint and remove list managers for the mailing lists of their domains.

Administrators can additionally

  • manage all domains, accounts and mailing lists,
  • create and delete domains,
  • appoint and remove domain managers,
  • manage the TLS policies and the blacklist of the mail server.

Only enabled mailboxes can be list or domain managers, but they can be mailboxes of any domain. When a manager's account is disabled, their manager roles are suspended until the account is enabled again. When it is deleted, all its manager roles are revoked.

Administrators are appointed in the configuration of the Post Office (see first steps). They can't be deleted, and only other administrators can edit them.
